During the COVID-19 period in 2021 and 2022, VMV’s early Helping Hand initiative connected with the diaspora community to carry out relief projects.

Support included dry rations for the villages of Vallan, Mavuththidal and Veeramamlai, assistance for pregnant mothers, aid for elderly people and help for school students.

In April 2022, the organisation began a significant child welfare savings scheme by depositing Rs. 25,000 each for 25 children from the villages. This marked a shift from emergency relief toward long-term care for children and education.
