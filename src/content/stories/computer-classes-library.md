To improve students’ technological knowledge, computer classes were started on 26 January 2024 through the support of diaspora donors.

The centre also opened Pungudi Arivagam, a library built with 1,800 books sent by VMV donors. In line with the saying “Reading makes a full man,” the library continues to expand and benefit students at Sulakshana Free Education Centre.

Together, computer learning and library access help students build confidence beyond the standard classroom curriculum.
