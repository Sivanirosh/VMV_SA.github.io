VMV Udhavikkaram stepped beyond education into wider social structure by forming the VMV Youth Wing under VMV administration.

The youth wing has carried out community activities such as roadside cleaning, shramadhanam, tree planting and street-light installation with donor support.

VMV also works to improve the physical and mental health of students. A grand athletic talent competition involving students in Pungudutivu was organised as part of this wider focus on wellbeing and community confidence.
